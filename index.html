    Good questions

    Is it free?

    Yes, The Scene is free. Tickets are sold by the venues, and the app links you straight to them.

    Where does it work?

    We’re starting in Upstate South Carolina: Anderson, Greenville, Clemson, Spartanburg and the towns around them. When you travel, you can search any city.

    Do my friends need the app?

    No. Your group text comes with a link that opens the event right in their phone’s browser.

    When do I get in?

    We’re inviting people in small groups. Join the list and we’ll email you once, when your invite is ready. Want a look now? Try the app.
