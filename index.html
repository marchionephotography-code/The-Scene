Three taps to a plan

  1. Pick a mood

    Food and drink, date night, a night out, something chill. Tap one and the list reshuffles.

  2. See what’s on near you

    Shows, games, library nights and local spots, with the weekend’s weather right there.

  3. Bring your people

    One group text to the whole crew, with the link, the time and the place.
